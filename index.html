🐪 How the Puzzle Works

The Goal: Transport as many bananas as possible from an Oasis (left) to a Market (right) across the desert.

📜 Rules

  • The camel eats 1 banana per km traveled — whether loaded or returning empty.
  • The camel has a limited carrying capacity.
  • You can create depots along the way to store bananas for later trips.

🧠 Optimal Strategy

  • When bananas exceed the capacity, the camel must make multiple round-trips.
  • Each phase moves bananas until the total drops to the next multiple of capacity.
  • With n trips needed, each km costs 2n − 1 bananas (n forward + n−1 return trips).
  • Fewer trips per phase = cheaper travel. The final leg needs only 1 trip.

🎮 Controls

  • Step — Execute one move of the plan.
  • Auto Play — Animate the full solution automatically.
  • Stop — Pause auto mode.
  • Reset — Start over with current settings.
  • Use sliders to change bananas, distance, and capacity.

With 3000 bananas, 1000 km, capacity 1000: the optimal delivery is 833 bananas.
